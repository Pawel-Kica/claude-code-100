name: visual
description: "Chart or image in chat, trigger 'generate me a visual'"
---

Answer what we're talking about with something to look at, shown directly in the chat.

Use whatever fits best: a chart, a diagram, a screenshot of a website or app, a generated image. Render it to a PNG in `/tmp`.

Take your time, and don't hesitate to take actions (e.g. search, research) to gather the data you need, because a fast wrong answer is just useless.

Look at the PNG yourself before sending it, and fix any overlapping labels or cut-off parts.

Put it in the reply as a Markdown image with the absolute path, `![what it shows](/tmp/.../file.png)`. T3 Code shows it inline.
